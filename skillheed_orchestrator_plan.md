## Combined Output (Validated, Non-duplicative, Business-first)

### 1) 90-Day Business Impact Plan

#### A. Growth (Top of Funnel)
- **SEO Agent**: Build a topic cluster around “AI learning paths”, “mentor-led upskilling”, and “job-ready LMS”.
- **Blog + Content Agent**: Publish 3 conversion-focused pillar posts/month and 8 supporting long-tail articles/month.
- **Social Media + Ads Agent**: Repurpose each pillar into 6 short-form assets and 3 paid creatives.
- **Expected outcome**: +35% qualified organic sessions and -18% blended CAC in 90 days.

#### B. Activation (Signup to First Value)
- **Funnel + UX + Conversion Agents**: Simplify onboarding to 3 steps: goal select → skill baseline → recommended learning path.
- **A/B Testing Agent**: Test onboarding CTA copy, progress bar visibility, and social proof cards.
- **Analytics Agent**: Track drop-offs at each onboarding step with daily anomaly alerts.
- **Expected outcome**: +20% increase in users reaching “first lesson started” within 24 hours.

#### C. Retention (Week 1 to Month 3)
- **Retention + Notification + Email Agents**: Deploy behavior-triggered nudges for inactive users at 24h, 72h, and day 7.
- **Community Growth Agent**: Introduce weekly cohort challenges and mentor AMA loops.
- **Feedback Analysis Agent**: Weekly churn-intent clustering (difficulty, lack of time, unclear path, poor mentor fit).
- **Expected outcome**: +12 point lift in D30 retention.

#### D. Revenue (Trial to Paid)
- **Pricing Strategy Agent**: Test 3-tier pricing (Starter / Pro / Career+Mentor).
- **CRM + Email Agents**: Lifecycle playbooks by segment (student, working professional, job seeker).
- **Conversion Agent**: Value-focused pricing page with proof blocks and ROI calculator.
- **Expected outcome**: +15% trial-to-paid conversion and +10% ARPU.

#### E. Product Quality (Trust + NPS)
- **Bug Detection + Performance + Security Agents**: Weekly reliability sprint (P0/P1 bugs, API latency hotspots, auth hardening).
- **Support Agent**: Macro-driven triage + escalation matrix for critical failures.
- **Expected outcome**: 40% reduction in repeated support tickets and improved NPS.

## Quality Validation Checklist (Orchestrator Gate)

Before shipping any agent output:
1. **Accuracy**: Validate claims against analytics or approved sources.
2. **Consistency**: Align messaging across ad, landing page, email, and product UI.
3. **No duplication**: Single owner per deliverable; shared reusable asset library.
4. **Security-safe**: API-only actions, no direct DB operations, no sensitive data exposure.
5. **Outcome-linked**: Every task tied to one KPI and a target delta.
